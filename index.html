About Me

Precision meets purpose

"Numbers tell a story — I make sure it's the right one."

I'm Reshu Jain, a CA Finalist registered with the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI). I cleared Class XII with a stellar 96.6% topping my school, and have since cleared every CA level — Foundation, Intermediate (both groups), and select Final papers — all in the first attempt, with exemptions across subjects.

I completed 2 years of articleship training at Minesh Anand & Associates and J Prateek & Co, gaining real-world exposure in audit, GST, income tax, financial reporting, and corporate compliance.

With my CA Final exams due in 2027, I'm passionate about ethical finance, strategic thinking, and continuous learning — always looking to bridge the gap between numbers and business decisions.
